Screenshots for the `shaders@0.1` draft PR (plugin view backgrounds), taken from a `ci` build of
`plug/shaders-tools` in an isolated instance, Dark theme, 1280x720 at 150 %.

- none.jpg: no background effect (the theme's own).
- dusk.jpg: docs/plugin-examples/dusk.
- half.jpg: dusk returning alpha 0.5; each pixel is the screen-value midpoint of none and dusk.
- panorama.jpg, panorama-turned.jpg: docs/plugin-examples/panorama with a generated lat/long grid
  picture (horizon yellow), level and orbited down.
- prefs-row.jpg: the earlier Preferences > Appearance > Background effect row (replaced).
- plugin-card.jpg: Preferences > Plugins, 360 Galaxy's background switched on under its card (the galaxy behind).
- plugin-card-switched.jpg: Dusk's switch turned on; the galaxy's turns off, and the view shows dusk.
- demo-galaxy.jpg, demo-rain.jpg: demo only, two looks built as shaders@0.1 plugins (a 360 galaxy
  picture, rain on a window). They are not part of the PR and are not published with it.

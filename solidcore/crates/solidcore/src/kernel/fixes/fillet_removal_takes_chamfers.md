`SmBrep::DeleteFilletFace` also removes a chamfer, a face whose edges to the faces it was cut between are not tangent: a four-sided one tries each pair of opposite edges as the sides (the faces across them extended meet in the sharp edge), each try rolled back when it fails; a ring all the way round a hole's or boss's rim takes closed rails that are not tangent. Where the two faces, extended, do not meet near the face (a chamfer between parallel faces), the removal fails as upstream's. Upstream takes only faces whose sides are tangent (own design, SolidCraft's direct modelling roadmap DM-0.3; not in the fork).
